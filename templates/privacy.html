Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

What we process

AethelConverts processes media files uploaded by visitors only to perform the requested conversion. We do not need an account to use the converter.

File handling

Uploaded files are stored temporarily while a conversion is running. The application is designed to remove the original upload after processing. Converted files are stored temporarily so the download can complete. A production deployment should also run a scheduled cleanup job for old output files.

Analytics and advertising

If advertising or analytics services are added, those providers may process information according to their own policies. The site should disclose the providers actually used before launch.

Children

AethelConverts is a general-purpose utility. Do not upload personal, confidential, or sensitive material.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method published by the site owner on the live website.
